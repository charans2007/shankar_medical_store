How to Reach Our Store

Conveniently located in central Malleshwaram with quick connectivity from across Bengaluru.

🚇

Namma Metro

Mantri Square Sampige Road (Green Line)

Mantri Square Sampige Road on the Green Line is the nearest metro station to our store. It is approximately a 6–8 minute walk (~600m) or a quick 2-minute auto ride to 7th Cross.

🚇 Nearest Metro: Green Line (~600m)
🚌

BMTC Bus Service

Malleshwaram Circle Bus Stop

Malleshwaram Circle is the nearest BMTC bus stop to our store. Frequent city buses run directly to this stop from Majestic (Kempegowda Bus Station), Shivajinagar, Yeshwanthpur, and KR Market.

🚏 Malleshwaram Circle (Short Walk)
🛵

Road & Parking

7th Cross, Sampige Road Junction

Convenient two-wheeler parking directly outside our store. Four-wheeler street parking is accessible along the adjacent 7th and 8th Cross side roads.
